# Admin website

Path: `D:\CA\Web`. Desktop-first and usable on a narrower window: the sidebar becomes a horizontal scroller.

Login is a split page with the Suva Voice story and the sign-in form. After login the shell has the brand sidebar, search, the date, and the staff member.

Dashboard cards cover customers, elderly people, today's check-ins, the next call, emergency contacts, reminders, unread alerts, completed calls, missed calls, and pending orders. Below that are the seven-day answered/missed chart, recent audit activity, reminders, order status counts, and monthly successful payments.

Call History starts a demo call for a selected person and shows the same keypad controls as the family app. Alerts can be moved through reviewing, contacted, and resolved, and the escalation list shows who was notified.

Care Orders assigns a provider, advances status, and includes doctor appointments and transport. Payments are a demo ledger in rupees. Subscriptions can be created and activated by an administrator. Reports and support are separate pages. Settings change retry attempts, retry delay, escalation delay, and whether the scheduler is enabled.

Only `admin` and `staff` tokens can stay on this site. A family login is rejected with a message to use the mobile app.
