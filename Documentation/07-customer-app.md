# Customer application

Path: `D:\CA\App`. Expo app, mobile-first, also rendered in a phone-width column on the web.

Screens: splash, onboarding, register, OTP, login, forgot password, home, loved ones, loved-one profile and edit, call history, live check-in, alert detail, reminders, care orders, order tracking, payments, subscriptions, notifications, profile, settings, and help.

Home shows the greeting, today's completed check-ins, emergency status, loved ones, reminders, recent alerts, recent orders, and shortcuts to a call, a reminder, and an order.

The live call screen lists loved ones, starts a demo call, shows the IVR sentence, and offers:

- Press 1 — I am okay
- Press 2 — I need assistance
- Press 3 — Emergency
- Simulate no answer
- Simulate failed call
- Simulate no response

Tabs are Home, Loved, Orders, Alerts, and More. Buttons are at least 48 pixels tall. Emergency alerts use a pink card and a text label, not colour alone.
